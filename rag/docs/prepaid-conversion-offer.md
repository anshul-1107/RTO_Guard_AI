# Prepaid Conversion Offer Policy

## Purpose
Converting a COD order to prepaid removes almost all RTO risk. We offer a small instant discount if the customer pays online before dispatch.

## Standard offer
MEDIUM-risk COD orders may receive a 5% discount, capped at ₹150, if paid via UPI or card within 12 hours. This offer can be sent automatically without human approval.

## Enhanced offer
HIGH-risk COD orders may receive up to 10% discount, capped at ₹250. Any offer above the standard 5% requires approval from the Ops Lead before it is sent.

## Eligibility rules
The offer is not valid on orders below ₹499. Only one prepaid offer may be sent per order. The combined discount on an order (sale discount plus prepaid offer) must never exceed 50%. If the order already has a sale discount above 45%, no prepaid offer is sent.

## Payment link
The payment link expires after 12 hours. If it expires unpaid, the order continues as COD and follows the COD Order Verification SOP.
