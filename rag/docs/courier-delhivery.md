# Delhivery Courier Terms (Kavya Threads contract)

## Overview
Most serviceable network; default partner for Tier 2 and Tier 3 pincodes.

## Delivery attempts
Delhivery makes up to 3 delivery attempts before marking a shipment for RTO.

## NDR response window
Kavya Threads must respond to a Delhivery NDR within 24 hours. If no response is received in time, Delhivery automatically initiates RTO.

## RTO charges
RTO is charged at 18% of the forward shipping charge, in addition to the forward charge already paid.

## COD remittance
COD amounts collected by Delhivery are remitted to Kavya Threads every 7 days.
