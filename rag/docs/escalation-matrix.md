# Escalation and Approval Matrix

## Actions allowed without approval
Sending a WhatsApp verification message, sending an IVR call, sending the standard 5% prepaid offer, requesting address correction, and placing an order on hold for up to 24 hours.

## Actions requiring Ops Lead approval
Any prepaid offer above 5%, cancelling an order for risk, waiving the repeat RTO rule, and shipping a HIGH-risk order after an expired hold.

## Actions requiring Ops Manager approval
Blocking a customer from future COD orders, and any change to discount caps.

## Response time
The Ops Lead must respond to approval requests within 2 hours during working hours (9:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST). If no response, the order stays on hold.
