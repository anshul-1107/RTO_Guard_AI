# Customer Communication Guidelines

## Tone
Messages must be warm, short, and respectful. The customer should feel we are confirming their order to serve them better, not accusing them. Never use words like "suspicious", "fraud", "risk", or "blacklist".

## What never to reveal
Never mention risk scores, the RTO model, past RTO counts, or internal reason codes in customer messages.

## Language
Default to English. If the delivery state is Hindi-speaking (Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Rajasthan, Delhi), send the message in simple Hinglish. Keep messages under 60 words.

## Required elements
Every message must include the customer's first name, the order ID, the order value, and a clear action (button or link). Promotional content must not be added to verification messages.

## Timing
Messages may only be sent between 9:00 AM and 9:00 PM IST.
