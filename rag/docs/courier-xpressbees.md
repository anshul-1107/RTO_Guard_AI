# Xpressbees Courier Terms (Kavya Threads contract)

## Overview
Lowest reverse shipping cost; good for low-value orders.

## Delivery attempts
Xpressbees makes up to 3 delivery attempts before marking a shipment for RTO.

## NDR response window
Kavya Threads must respond to a Xpressbees NDR within 24 hours. If no response is received in time, Xpressbees automatically initiates RTO.

## RTO charges
RTO is charged at 15% of the forward shipping charge, in addition to the forward charge already paid.

## COD remittance
COD amounts collected by Xpressbees are remitted to Kavya Threads every 7 days.
