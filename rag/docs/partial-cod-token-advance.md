# Partial COD Token Advance Policy

## What it is
Partial COD means the customer pays a small token amount online and the balance in cash on delivery. It strongly reduces refusals at the doorstep.

## Amount
The token advance is a flat ₹99 for all eligible orders.

## When it is used
Partial COD is mandatory for repeat RTO customers. It may also be offered to HIGH-risk COD orders above ₹1,999 as an alternative to the prepaid conversion offer. Only one of the two (prepaid offer or partial COD) may be offered on a single order.

## Payment window
The token must be paid within 12 hours. If unpaid, the order is held for the remaining hold window and then escalated to the Ops Lead.
