# Repeat RTO Customer Policy

## Definition
A repeat RTO customer is one with 2 or more RTO orders in their order history.

## Rule
Repeat RTO customers cannot place a pure COD order. Their COD orders are converted to Partial COD: the customer must pay a ₹99 token advance online, and the rest is collected on delivery. See the Partial COD Token Advance Policy.

## Communication
The message must not mention the customer's past RTOs. Frame the token advance as a standard confirmation step.

## Exceptions
The Ops Lead may waive the rule for customers with more than 10 successful deliveries.
