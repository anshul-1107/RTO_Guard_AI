# RTO and Logistics Glossary

## RTO (Return to Origin)
A shipment that could not be delivered and is sent back to the seller's warehouse. The seller pays both forward and reverse shipping.

## NDR (Non-Delivery Report)
A report raised by the courier when a delivery attempt fails, for example because the customer was unavailable, refused the order, or the address was incomplete.

## Reattempt
Another delivery attempt after an NDR. The seller must respond to an NDR within the courier's response window to request a reattempt.

## Partial COD
An order where the customer pays a token online and the balance on delivery.

## RTS (Return to Shipper)
Used interchangeably with RTO by some couriers.
