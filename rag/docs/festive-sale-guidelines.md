# Festive and Sale Season Guidelines

## Why sales are riskier
During sale events (Diwali, End of Season Sale, Republic Day Sale) impulse COD orders rise sharply and RTO rates typically increase.

## Tighter rules during sales
During an active sale, orders with sale discount above 30% placed between midnight and 5:00 AM are treated one risk band higher (LOW becomes MEDIUM, MEDIUM becomes HIGH).

## Offer limits during sales
The prepaid conversion offer is capped at 5% (maximum ₹150) during sales, even for HIGH-risk orders, because margins are already reduced.
