# Bluedart Courier Terms (Kavya Threads contract)

## Overview
Lowest RTO rate among partners; preferred for high-value orders. Limited Tier 3 coverage.

## Delivery attempts
Bluedart makes up to 3 delivery attempts before marking a shipment for RTO.

## NDR response window
Kavya Threads must respond to a Bluedart NDR within 48 hours. If no response is received in time, Bluedart automatically initiates RTO.

## RTO charges
RTO is charged at 25% of the forward shipping charge, in addition to the forward charge already paid.

## COD remittance
COD amounts collected by Bluedart are remitted to Kavya Threads every 10 days.
