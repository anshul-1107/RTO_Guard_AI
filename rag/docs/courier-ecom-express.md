# Ecom Express Courier Terms (Kavya Threads contract)

## Overview
Strong coverage in North and East India.

## Delivery attempts
Ecom Express makes up to 2 delivery attempts before marking a shipment for RTO.

## NDR response window
Kavya Threads must respond to a Ecom Express NDR within 24 hours. If no response is received in time, Ecom Express automatically initiates RTO.

## RTO charges
RTO is charged at 18% of the forward shipping charge, in addition to the forward charge already paid.

## COD remittance
COD amounts collected by Ecom Express are remitted to Kavya Threads every 7 days.
