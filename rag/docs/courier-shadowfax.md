# Shadowfax Courier Terms (Kavya Threads contract)

## Overview
Hyperlocal and same-day in metros; NDR window is short, so act fast.

## Delivery attempts
Shadowfax makes up to 2 delivery attempts before marking a shipment for RTO.

## NDR response window
Kavya Threads must respond to a Shadowfax NDR within 12 hours. If no response is received in time, Shadowfax automatically initiates RTO.

## RTO charges
RTO is charged at 20% of the forward shipping charge, in addition to the forward charge already paid.

## COD remittance
COD amounts collected by Shadowfax are remitted to Kavya Threads every 5 days.
